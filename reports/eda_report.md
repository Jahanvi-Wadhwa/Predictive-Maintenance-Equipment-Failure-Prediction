The target is heavily imbalanced, so accuracy alone is misleading. The pipeline therefore reports precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC and average precision (PR-AUC), and class imbalance is handled explicitly.

## 3. Leakage review

- `TWF`, `HDF`, `PWF`, `OSF`, `RNF` are the *components* of `Machine failure` (failure = any of them). They are excluded from model inputs and used only for analysis.
- `UDI` (row id) and `Product ID` (product code) are identifiers and are excluded.
